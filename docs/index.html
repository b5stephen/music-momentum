What it does

Built around the bit you can't play yet

Three tools on one screen, with the controls where your thumb already is.

Speed wheel

Spin the wheel to slow a song anywhere from 30% to full speed. Save the speed with the song, and tap to flip between your practice speed and the real thing.

30% – 100% · saved per song

Markers & clips

Mark a point to jump back to, or a clip with a start and an end. Name it, nudge it by a tenth of a second, and find it as a pill under the timeline.

Points & clips · 0.1s nudges

Loop chains

Loop one clip, several clips back to back, or the whole song. While the loop runs, tap pills to add or drop clips without missing a beat.

One clip · a chain · the whole song

How it works

From "how does that go?" to playing along

  1. 1

    Pick a song

    Search Apple Music or browse your own library. Music Momentum only asks for access when you first reach for a song.

  2. 2

    Find your speed

    Turn the wheel until the part is playable, then save that speed with the song for next time.

  3. 3

    Mark it and loop it

    Mark the bars you're stuck on as clips and loop them, one or several in a row, until they stick.

iCloud sync

Your practice follows you

Saved songs, their speeds and every marker sync through your own private iCloud, so whichever device you pick up is where you left off.

  • Opens on the song you were last practising
  • Keeps the screen awake while a song plays
  • No sign-up: it uses the Apple Account you already have
